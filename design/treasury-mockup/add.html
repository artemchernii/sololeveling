How it behaves

  • Each button adds a row of its kind, coloured by kind — in green, out red, transfer lavender, buy blue, sell amber. Add as many as you want, of any kinds; ✕ removes one.
  • Everything is prefilled: your only account (or the one you used last), its currency, today. The amount is focused; Enter adds another row of the same kind.
  • Where it came from / what it was: a dropdown of what you already use, with + new… to make your own — kept for next time.
  • This is the same row a statement or screenshot becomes after reading — same badge, same dropdowns, prefilled by the reader instead of by you.
  • Only what is missing is marked, and the button says what will be added: "add 3 · +€2,140 −€48".
