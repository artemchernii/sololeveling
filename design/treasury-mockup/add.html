What changed from the last one

  • The app's own icons and pills: the buttons are the quiet pills you already have; only the icon carries the kind. In rows, a soft badge in the app's tints — green in, red out, lavender for transfers and trades.
  • One grid for every kind — badge, account, what, amount, day, ✕ — so the fields line up down the list, whatever the mix. On a phone each row folds into three lines.
  • No browser dropdowns, date pickers or alerts. Every field opens our own list: search at the top, icons, and "+ create “…”" when what you type is new — kept for next time.
  • Buy and sell search real tickers (Yahoo, as the app does now) with their logos — stocks, ETFs, crypto; gold by the gram.
  • The day is a quiet "today" with the calendar icon in grey; it opens today · yesterday · a picked day.
