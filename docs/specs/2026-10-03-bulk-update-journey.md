# Bulk update — the journey (3 Oct)

Artem, 3 Oct: "When I simply drag and drop multiple csv/pdf files, you
read and analyze which bank, what to update, I can review it and update
state of finances." He has four bank accounts and is about to bring in
every statement. This goes ahead of the A.6 fixes and Flow: the
statements answer most of A.6's questions.

Journey only. No layout, no code — those come after his yes.

## When he opens it

**Who and when.** Him, at the desk, a few times a year for a big catch-up
(today: a year of four banks), then roughly once a month: download last
month's statements, drop them in, done. Not daily; daily is ⌘L.

**What he wants to know:** "Did everything land, and is my money right
now?" Not a list of 400 rows: whether each account adds up, and what
changed.

**What he does:** drops the files, looks once, fixes what the app could
not know, presses one button, sees the Overview change.

## The steps, in his words

1. **"I drop everything."** CSVs, PDFs and screenshots, mixed, any order,
   on the ADD zone he already uses. Nothing to pick first: not the bank,
   not the account, not the month.
2. **"It figures out what each file is."** Per file: which bank and
   account (from the bank's name, IBAN or card digits inside it), the
   days it covers, its opening and closing balance. He watches each file
   go from reading to read, so a big drop never looks stuck. A file it
   cannot place says why, in one line, and asks one question ("Whose
   statement is this?" with his accounts to tap).
3. **"A bank I haven't added appears as a new account,"** named and with
   its logo, from what the file says. One tap to keep it. No form.
4. **"I see it per account, not per file."** One block per account:
   - the days now covered, and any hole between statements ("nothing
     between 3 and 31 Mar: a statement is missing")
   - how many rows are new and how many it already had (overlapping
     statements are normal and are not counted twice)
   - balance at the start → balance at the end, and whether the rows in
     between add up; where they do not, where the gap is (the A.5 check)
   - his money moving between his own accounts, paired across the
     files: "€500 BPI → ActivoBank, 1 Sep" (example). Both sides found in the
     same drop pair up before anything is saved.
   - the rows themselves behind a tap, never shown by default
5. **"I fix only what it couldn't know."** A wrong bank guess (tap the
   account), a move with no other side ("from you, but from which
   account?"), a gap. Everything else is already decided.
6. **"One button."** Apply everything, oldest statement first, so every
   balance lands on its day. He can leave one account out of it.
7. **"And I see the change."** Back on Overview, the total and the year
   line show the new picture; what it changed is said once: "4 accounts
   updated · 312 new rows · 9 moves paired · line now starts Oct 2025".

## What it is not

- Not a new page or sidebar entry: the ADD zone and one review sheet.
- Not a row-by-row review. Today's per-file review stays for a single
  file; the bulk review is the same reading, gathered per account.
- Nothing is saved before step 6. Closing the sheet keeps the drop to
  finish later.
