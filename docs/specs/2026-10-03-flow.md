## What gets built

The Flow room is rebuilt to the mockup, replacing today's Bills card,
MonthMoney and the calendar.

1. **The month line** on top: in, out, left this month (moves not
   counted), six months of in/out bars, last month in one sentence.
2. **Three tabs**: AHEAD (default) · SPENDING · MOVEMENTS. They stay put
   while the page scrolls, and each tab carries one line saying what's in it.
3. **AHEAD**: free cash with everything taken out by default (bills and salary
   on their days, plus everyday spending as a range from the cheapest to the
   dearest of the last three full months), toggle "only bills and salary";
   this month's bills in full (paid ✓ with the statement row, still to
   come, not enough on the day); later months folded to what differs;
   free cash, everything else and subscriptions beside it; + BILL; "what I
   pay this year".
4. **SPENDING**: groups (categories) for a chosen month, six small bars
   each, what grew most in one sentence; tap a group for its rows.
5. **MOVEMENTS**: every account in one list, newest first, by day; a move
   between his accounts is one grey row; account chips, search, a day
   strip; tap a row → the row sheet with REPEATS.

## Bills, found and added

- **The key.** A payee is recognised by the line the bank printed
  (`meta.raw`), not the display name, which drifts ("Vodafone",
  "PAG. 919703708 - VODAFONE"). `src/lib/payee.ts` makes the key: upper
  case, words with digits and bank noise (DD, PAG, TRF, SEPA, CR, To,
  Card) dropped, first three words.
- **Found.** `src/lib/findBills.ts`: same key in two of the last three
  months, one payment a month (twice a month is not a monthly bill:
  Solinca, Vodafone top-ups), amounts within 10%, days within 3. Money
  out from any account, money in only into a bank. Broker rows and moves
  are left out.
  `recurring.find` writes each one as a `recurring` row with `matchKey`
  and `foundAt` (NEW for seven days). It runs when Flow opens and after
  an UPDATE ALL applies.
- **Not a bill.** `recurring.notBill` sets `refusedAt`; its key is never
  found again and the row leaves every view.
- **From a row.** `recurring.fromRow(logId, cadence)`: name, amount,
  day, month and account come from the row.
- **Suggestions.** `recurring.likely`: payees in the last twelve months
  that are not bills yet, not refused, and outside everyday groups
  (groceries, eating out, transport, shopping, clothes, fun), newest first.
- **Paid** is worked out each time, nothing written back: a row this
  month with the bill's key and an amount within 15%, or
  `meta.recurringId`. Nothing is written onto the row.

## Numbers (CLAUDE.md, four sources)

- In / out / groups / six months: sums of logged euro rows (source 1),
  each opening its rows.
- Free cash: latest balances (source 2), banks and cash, brokers out.
- AHEAD points: free cash ± his bills and salary on their days. The range
  subtracts, per day, the cheapest and the dearest of the last three
  full months' non-bill spending (each a real sum, shown beside). Worked
  out in `aggregate.ahead`; the page only draws it.
