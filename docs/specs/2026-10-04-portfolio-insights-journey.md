## When he opens Portfolio

**Who and when.** Him, a few times a week — after a buy, when the
market moved, or to decide where this month's money goes.

**What he wants to know**, in this order:

1. "What do I own, everywhere?" — shares, ETFs **and crypto**, one list.
2. "Am I up or down, and on what?" — per position, in euros, since bought.
3. "What is this thing?" — one position opened: its price over time,
   what I paid, what it is (company, ETF, coin), its logo right.
4. "Where should the next euro go?" — answered in Insights, against his
   own plan, never by the app.

## The steps, in his words

1. **Crypto is its own account.** "Revolut Crypto" (Revolut Digital
   Assets, Cyprus) beside Trade Republic and Trading 212, counted in
   INVESTED (his pick, 3 Oct). Read from his statement PDF: the five
   coins he holds (SOL, ETH, BTC, XRP, ADA), and the ~90 trades since
   2020 for what he paid. Staking rewards are coins at €0 cost — never
   "money in". Prices: a daily close (BTC-EUR …), stored and dated, like
   his shares.
2. **A position opened.** The mock built on 3 Oct
   (design/treasury-mockup/stock.html, five situations) is the start —
   he marks it up, it changes until he says go.
3. **Logos that are right.** Today a logo is guessed from the ticker,
   so VUAA and SHLD show US companies. A logo comes from what the thing
   is: the company's site, the ETF's issuer, the coin's mark; letters
   when unknown.

## When he opens Insights

**What he wants:** to see his money against **his own plan** — how much
he earns and keeps, what he invests each month, how his split looks
against the split he chose. The plan is his, written once (a target
split, a monthly amount, his own rules); the app shows reality beside
it. It never says what to buy and invents no score.

**What he does:** reads it once a month after payday, maybe changes his
plan, goes to Portfolio to act.
