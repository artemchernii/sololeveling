# Trading 212 connected

Mockup: `design/treasury-mockup/trading-212.html` (ten situations, both
widths). "go" on 12 Oct, after two marks: the changed block reads in euros
with the shares small underneath ("amount is important, do you think I
memorize what is the value of each company?"), and each changed amount has
an arrow up or down. Nothing is built.

## Journey (12 Oct, "journey yes for now, but I will review again after

implementation, because some things you say is not clear")

You open Finances a few times a week and drop nothing for Trading 212. Its
card shows the cash and the investments as Trading 212 reports them, and
when: "from Trading 212 · today 14:05". When you bought or sold since the
last look, a block on the card says what changed in euros — what each
company was worth at the last look, and now — until you close it with ✕.
Connecting is one press: you make the key in the 212 app, put it on the
backend, and press CONNECT on the card. There is no bank page to visit.

At review, say each choice below again in plainer words, on the screen.

## What Trading 212 gives (its OpenAPI file, 12 Oct; no real call made)

- Base `https://live.trading212.com` (demo: `https://demo.trading212.com`).
  HTTP Basic, `key:secret`. Invest and Stocks ISA only.
- `GET /api/v0/equity/account/summary` — `cash.availableToTrade`,
  `cash.inPies`, `cash.reservedForOrders`, `currency`, `totalValue`,
  `investments.currentValue / totalCost`. 1 call per 5 s.
- `GET /api/v0/equity/positions` — per position `instrument.isin / ticker /
name / currency`, `quantity`, `averagePricePaid`, `currentPrice`,
  `walletImpact.currency / currentValue / totalCost`. 1 call per second.
- History (`/equity/history/orders`, `/dividends`, `/transactions`,
  20 a minute, 50 a page, `nextPagePath`) — the next slice, not this one.
- 401/403 = the key is not accepted; 408/429/5xx = not reached.
- Read-only cannot be seen from the API: permissions are ticked in the 212
  app when the key is made. Whether a key expires is not known.

## What is kept

The account card, `AccountFoot`, the `Sheet`, the landed ring, the
"checking…" lavender edge, the check on opening Finances when the last
answer is over 4 hours old (`Accounts.tsx`), `bankLinks`, `bank.links`.
UPDATE stays on the Trading 212 card: the history file still comes that way.

## Scenarios (each becomes a test; Trading 212 is canned, never his)

- S1 as today: the card reads from the last file; CONNECT beside UPDATE.
- S2 CONNECT → three facts → connect → "asking Trading 212…" at once →
  "Trading 212 is connected" with N the same / N changed → Done closes; the
  footer says "from Trading 212 · now", green links beside free cash and
  investments.
- S3 no key on the backend → "The key is not on the backend yet. Nothing
  changed." and the two names; no link is kept.
- S4 connected and fresh: stored numbers, no request at render.
- S5 opening Finances with the last answer over 4 h old starts one check
  ("checking Trading 212… · last 08:02"); a second open inside 4 h none.
- S6 bought and sold: saved with no press; the block grows in — "3 changed",
  "as Trading 212 shows", per line the euros at the last look and now with
  an arrow (green up, red down), shares small underneath, free cash as a
  line; ✕ closes it and it stays closed. The same answer again writes
  nothing and shows nothing.
- S7 twenty changed: three lines and "17 more", which opens them all.
- S8 a share with no market price: saved with Trading 212's price, the line
  says "price from Trading 212". No question.
- S9 cash only: "investments · none held".
- S10 key not accepted (401/403): amber, "Trading 212 did not accept the
  key", "cash and positions of today, 08:02", TRY AGAIN; nothing written.
- S11 not reached (timeout, 429, 5xx): the same with "did not answer".
- Every write: pressed → busy words at once → landed → closes.
- Refusals (convex-test): another owner's account; an account that is not
  Trading 212; a check while one runs; an answer with half the calls
  failed writes nothing.
- Phone width; dark and light.

## Data — where each number comes from

- **Free cash** is an external reading (source 4): `cash.availableToTrade`
  and `inPies` and `reservedForOrders` added (his "yes", 12 Oct), stored
  through `writeBalance` with `source: 'sync'`, `recordedAt` the moment
  212 answered.
- **Positions** are stored looks: one `holdings` row per position
  (`shares` = `quantity`, `paidEur` = `walletImpact.totalCost` when the
  wallet currency is EUR), all with one `asOf`. Matched by ISIN first.
- **Investments and each line's euros** are what the app already shows:
  shares × the app's stored daily price (`aggregate.readPositions`). 212's
  `currentPrice` is stored as a price only where the app has none (the
  path `writeScreenPrice` takes for a frozen share today).
- **The changed block**: per instrument, shares at the last look and now,
  kept on the link; the euros are worked out by `aggregate` from those
  shares and stored prices — at the last look's day, and now. Free cash:
  the two stored balances. Nothing typed, nothing invented.
- **Checked at / last failure / why** are facts about the link.
- The key and secret are Convex environment variables he sets himself:
  `TRADING212_KEY`, `TRADING212_SECRET`. Never returned, logged or stored.

## How it would be built

Three PRs, each from master (the engine came to 1,231 lines with its
tests, so it is two): the calls and what they say (`t212Api.ts`,
`src/lib/t212.ts`), then the storing (`t212.ts`, the link's fields, the
mocks), then the card.

1. **Engine, no screen** — `convex/t212Api.ts` (two calls, canned answers
   when `E2E=1`, like `bankApi.ts`), `convex/t212.ts` (`connect` action;
   `check` action; `begin` → `arrive` → `fail`; `close`), a pure mapper in
   `src/lib/t212.ts` (212's answer → holding rows, what changed), fields on
   `bankLinks` (`via: 't212'`, `changed`, `failed`), `bank.links` returns
   them, mock steps in `convex/e2eBank.ts`. convex-test and unit tests.
2. **The card** — `AccountFoot` (from Trading 212 / checking / the two
   amber lines / CONNECT where `institution` is Trading 212),
   `AccountChanged.tsx` (the block), the connect window (in `BankSheet` or
   beside it if that passes 500 lines), the check on opening in
   `Accounts.tsx`, `e2e/trading-212.spec.ts`, the journeys row.

Settled in code, 12 Oct:

- How a position leaves: a holding missing from the newest look is only
  flagged (`notSeenSince`), never zeroed — a screenshot may be half a list.
  Trading 212's list is whole, so a company that left it is written as a
  look of 0 shares; `reconcile` then reads it as sold.
- "Cash only" follows from that: every company held gets a look of 0.
- The rows are written directly, with no intake: there is no file to take
  back. An unchanged answer writes nothing; a file saved after the link's
  last look makes the next answer write every position again.
- The first answer shows no changed block: what a file had wrong is not
  something he bought or sold. The window says N the same / N changed.
- `institution` is `'trading-212'` (`src/lib/institutions.ts`).

## Out of scope

Orders, dividends, deposits as rows (next slice — then UPDATE leaves the
card and "since 1 Oct" follows). Placing or cancelling anything. Pies as
their own thing. The once-a-day check (bank slice 3). Disconnecting.

## Answered 12 Oct ("1 yes, 2 red, 3 yes")

1. **Money in pies and held for orders** is counted in free cash: it
   matches the 212 app's total cash, not only "available to trade".
2. **A sold company's arrow is red**: the amount in the account went down.
3. **A screenshot dropped for a connected card** is taken as today, and
   the next answer from Trading 212 replaces it.
