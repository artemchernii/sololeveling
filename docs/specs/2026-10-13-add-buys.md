# ADD BUYS on one position's page

Mockup: `design/treasury-mockup/add-buys.html` (:3950). Artem said "go"
to it on 13 Oct, after three marks (BUY as a badge, a green tick on a
finished step, the two buttons aligned and in caps) — and one more with
the go: the tiles are "still bland a bit" (see Look).

## Journey

He opens Gold and sees "bought for —": no buy of it was ever in a file
(checked 13 Oct: 3,771 trades stored, none for `GC=F`; only the ounces
from a Revolut screenshot). His words: "maybe we want to have a button
to update particular asset (stock/metal/crypto) like this screenshot of
transactions". Revolut's app lists one asset's buys on a screen of its
own; that screenshot is the file.

He presses ADD BUYS on the position's page, drops the screenshots
(several: the list scrolls), sees what was read beside what he holds,
saves. How often: once per asset whose buys are missing, then when he
buys more outside a file.

## What is there already (read before building)

- `convex/ai/intake.ts`: the reader returns `kind: 'trades'` rows from
  a screenshot; days with no year go through `yearlessToThisYear`
  (`src/lib/noYear.ts`); each trade name goes to `tickers.find` (Yahoo).
- The update window named for one account: `UpdateSheet` → `BulkUpdate`
  (`forAccount`), `intake.openBatch` / `startBatch` with `accountId`
  (journey map, "UPDATE on one account").
- The check of buys against shares held, per company:
  `src/lib/sharesCheck.ts`, `convex/sharesCheck.ts`, `BulkSharesCheck`.
- Rows already saved are skipped by the batch (`twinOf`).

## What is missing

1. A trade named for a metal (XAU, XAG, XPT, XPD) must become that
   metal's instrument (`METALS` in `src/lib/metals.ts`), not a Yahoo
   search. Units are troy ounces.
2. A door on the position's page, and a window told the instrument as
   well as the account: rows for any other asset are not saved from it.
3. Not known: whether the reader reads "Buy XAU · 0,08275 units ·
   €3 607,63" as a buy with comma decimals. One paid reading of his
   screenshot answers it — do it first; a canned copy of the answer is
   the test mock.

## Screens and files

`docs/journeys/finances.md`.

## Scenarios (each an e2e test, mock data, canned reading)

- S1 the page of a thing with no buys → ADD BUYS under the tiles.
- S2 pressed → the window named for the thing, a drop area.
- S3 dropped → "reading" at once, rows as they arrive.
- S4 four buys read, fewer units than held → the three sums, "X oz of
  what is held is not in these buys", save still offered.
- S5 the buys reach what is held → the green line.
- S6 the same screenshot again → every row "already saved", nothing to
  save.
- S7 save → "saving…" at once, the landed tick, the tile fills, BUY dots
  on the chart; it closes.
- S8 a screenshot of another asset → its rows are not saved here; said
  plainly.
- S9 a failed save says so and writes nothing; a failed reading can be
  tried again.
- S10 phone width.
- convex-test: metals become their instrument; a second save of the same
  rows writes nothing; another user's position is refused.

## Look

Labels: BUY / SELL, never "bought" / "sold". The tile is "buy total"
(his question on "bought for"; "buy price" reads as a price per unit).
A missing part is a state: amber, with an icon and a pulse, not grey
words — "bland is a bug". While buys are missing, "since bought" stays
empty: a gain against part of what was paid would be a wrong number.

## Data

Every number is a sum of trade rows (source 1) or the units of the last
broker screenshot (source 2). The missing units are the two shown side
by side, both always on screen. No progress bar: no goal gives it a
denominator.

## Out of scope

Sells typed by hand, editing a saved buy, the portfolio helper.
