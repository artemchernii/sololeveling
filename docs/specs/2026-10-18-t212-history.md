# Trading 212 history

Mockup: `design/treasury-mockup/t212-history.html` (six situations, both
widths). Nothing is built; waits for his "go".

## Journey (his words, from the 212 and bank sessions)

You open Finances a few times a week and drop nothing for Trading 212. Its
card already reads cash and positions from Trading 212 (12 Oct). What it
does not have is what happened: "since 1 Oct · no trades yet" while you
bought. Now each check brings Trading 212's own rows — buys and sells,
dividends, interest, fees, money in and out — and the card says what came:
`2 new · as Trading 212 shows`, the rows with their badges, ✕ to close
(the bank's arrived block, 11 Oct). `bought since 1 Oct` reads the buys, beside Revolut's `spent since 1 Oct`.

Money you send from Revolut or ActivoBank is a move, not spending ("deposits
to broker acc … what is spending", 10 Oct): one `TRANSFER` with
`saved in Revolut · 16 Oct` on the 212 card, `saved in Trading 212 · 16 Oct` on Revolut's. With no bank row saved for it,
it is `Deposit` · `not saved in a bank` (212 does not name the bank) and pairs by itself when the bank row arrives.
A dividend is money in: `DIVIDEND`, the company's logo, the euros, the
shares under it. Never a salary.

Opening the card shows the rows by day, the same lines, the files' rows
beside them (`already added · csv 12 Oct`). No UPDATE, no file.

## What Trading 212 gives (its OpenAPI file, read 18 Oct; no real call)

- `GET /api/v0/equity/history/orders` — per item `order` (id, side BUY/SELL,
  status, instrument isin/ticker/name/currency, filledQuantity, filledValue)
  and `fill` (id, filledAt, price, quantity, type TRADE / STOCK_SPLIT / …,
  `walletImpact` currency, netValue, fxRate, taxes). 20 a minute.
- `GET /api/v0/equity/history/dividends` — amount, amountInEuro, currency,
  instrument, paidOn, quantity, grossAmountPerShare, reference, type. 20 a
  minute.
- `GET /api/v0/equity/history/transactions` — amount, currency, dateTime,
  reference, type DEPOSIT / WITHDRAW / FEE / TRANSFER /
  INTEREST_ON_FREE_CASH / LENDING_INTEREST. 20 a minute.
- All three: newest first, `limit` ≤ 50, `cursor`, `nextPagePath` null on
  the last page. Each item has its own id (`fill.id`, `reference`).

## Cost (Convex free plan, 1 GB/month)

Three more GETs per check; a check runs only on opening Finances when the
last is over 4 h old (≤ ~5 a day) — Trading 212's calls are free. The first
answer writes the rows after the newest file row once (~50 rows, ~30 KB);
after that 0–5 rows a check, and an answer with nothing new writes nothing.
Reads per check: the 212 account's rows since the last one through
`by_owner_account_time` plus the banks' moves ±3 days (a few KB). The card
adds the block's three lines and counts (~1 KB). Under 100 KB a day.

## Scenarios (each a test; Trading 212 is canned, never his)

- S1 as today: no history yet → card unchanged.
- S2 first answer: only rows after the newest saved row of the account; the
  overlap day's rows that a file already wrote are `already added`, not
  written; `14 new · 3 already added`; three lines and `11 more`, which
  opens the account.
- S3 a buy and a dividend: a `trades` row (buy, shares, €/share from
  `walletImpact.netValue`) and an `income` log (dividend, `amountInEuro`);
  `bought since 1 Oct` reads `€300.00`; ✕ closes and it stays closed.
- S4 deposit, bank side saved: a `move` row, paired (`pairOf`) with the
  Revolut row of the same amount within 3 days; both cards say
  `saved in <other account> · <day>`. Not counted as spent or earned.
- S5 deposit, bank side not saved: the 212 move is `Deposit` · `not saved in a bank`;
  the bank's next rows pair it with no question.
- S6 the same answer again writes nothing and shows nothing new (ids kept).
- S7 interest → `income`; fee → `expense` in the 212 account; a split →
  the existing `split` trade.
- S8 an order not filled (cancelled, rejected) → nothing.
- S9 not reached / key rejected: the footer's two amber lines, as today;
  nothing written.
- Refusals (convex-test): another owner; an account that is not 212; a
  check while one runs; a page that failed half way writes nothing.
- Phone width; dark and light.

## Data — where each number comes from

- Rows are logged rows (source 1) and trades (the sum rule over trades);
  every euro is Trading 212's, in the account's currency, never derived.
- The block's counts are counts of the rows that answer wrote.
- Each row keeps Trading 212's id so an answer is never written twice.

## Out of scope

Pies. Withdrawals to a bank other than his. Tax lines. Older history than
the files (asked below). The positions' changed block is kept as is.

## Questions (each with my recommendation)

1. **First answer: from where?** Only after the newest row the files
   already saved (the bank rule, 11 Oct) — recommended; older history is in
   the csv already. Or the whole history, matched row by row.
2. **The changed block and the new rows on one card?** Rows explain a buy
   better than "8 → 10 shares". Recommended: when an answer brings rows,
   the rows block shows and the changed block does not; the changed block
   stays for a change no row explains.
3. **A dividend in Flow**: money in, listed with income, never a salary —
   recommended. Or kept out of Flow, only on the account.
