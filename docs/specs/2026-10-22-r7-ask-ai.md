# R7 — Ask AI

Journey (draft, waits his yes): on any page he presses ⌘J (or ✦ ASK in
the top bar). A drawer opens from the right — the Insights ASK drawer's
shape. He types or taps a question about his own life: "What did I
actually do in August?", "How many workouts since 1 September?",
"Portuguese this month and last month", "Where did the money go last
week?", "Which projects got commits in October?". The answer is a
headline and 3–5 lines; under each line the app prints the numbers
(`WORKOUT · AUG 9`) and a tap opens the rows behind them. He reads it,
maybe asks a follow-up, and closes with Esc. A few times a month, mostly
at a month's end or when a period feels blurry.

Look: `design/ask-mockup/index.html` (launch config `ask-mockup`, :3970).

## What exists and is reused

- **The pipe** — Insights ASK (I4): on localhost `vite/claudeRead.ts`
  runs `claude -p` on his Pro plan; deployed, an action calls Sonnet 5.5
  with the API under the one $2/month cap (`aiSpend`, `addSpend`,
  `spentIn`, `readCost`). R7 adds one route (`/__claude/rows-ask`) and
  one action (`ai.rowsAsk.ask`) on the same helpers; no new model, price
  table or cap.
- **The answer shape** — Review's read and ASK: the model names fact
  keys, the app prints those facts; a key not given is dropped
  (`parseAsk`'s rule). Model, time, tokens, cost and payer on every
  answer. ⧉ COPY from `askCopy`.
- **The drawer** — `AskDrawer`'s anatomy (header with payer, thread,
  dock, NOT SAVED), mounted in the shell instead of in Insights.

## What R7 adds

- `src/lib/rowsAsk.ts`: `periodOf(question, today)` — the period from the
  words (a month name, "last week", "this month", "since 1 September",
  default the last 30 days); `rowFacts(rows, period)` — log counts and
  sums per kind and area, tasks done, commits per project, both months
  when two are named; `rowLines(rows)` — one compact line per row for
  the model to read (day, kind, area, text, value). Up to 92 days send
  rows; longer sends month counts only. Prompt, schema, canned answers.
- `convex/rowsAsk.ts`: `forPeriod` (internal query, owner index, bounded
  by `occurredAt`) — the rows and counts the action hands the model.
- The fact chips open their rows in the answer (the ids come back with
  the facts; nothing is fetched again).
- ⌘J in `_app.tsx` beside ⌘K and ⌘L; ✦ ASK in the TopBar.

## Scenarios

- S1 ⌘J opens the drawer: READS badges, five example questions; Esc and
  ✕ close it.
- S2 ask "What did I actually do in August?" → the question at once,
  READING ROWS with the period badge `AUG 1 – 31` → headline, lines,
  fact chips with the app's numbers, stamp (model · time · tokens · cost
  · payer).
- S3 tap a fact chip → its rows open under the line; ALL n → shows all.
- S4 a period with no rows → ❔ NO DATA line, nothing guessed.
- S5 the reader fails → ✕ NOT REACHED, $0 · NOTHING PAID, ↻ ASK AGAIN.
- S6 deployed at the cap → `$2 OF $2 · NEXT 1 NOV`, ASK off.
- S7 reload → thread gone (nothing stored).
- Unit: `periodOf` cases; `rowFacts` counts match `aggregate`'s for the
  same rows; a fact key not given is dropped; a number in a line that is
  in no fact is flagged.
- Convex: `forPeriod` returns only the owner's rows, only in the period.
- Phone: bottom sheet, no horizontal scroll.

## Data

Every number on screen is a log count or sum over a stated period, or an
entity count (sources 1 and 3), counted by the app and printed by the
app. The model's words may quote only those numbers. Nothing the model
says is stored; the thread lives on the page.

## Out of scope

Notes and Vault texts, writing anything (no "log this" from chat),
stored threads, a chat history page, voice.

## Questions (each with my pick)

1. Shortcut — **⌘J** plus ✦ ASK in the top bar (⌘K and ⌘L are taken).
2. Rows it reads — **logs (money too), tasks done, projects, commits,
   goals**; not notes or the Vault.
3. Kept — **not saved**: the thread lives until reload, like Insights.
4. Cost — **Sonnet 5.5**, free on localhost (Pro); deployed about
   $0.03–0.06 a question from the same $2 a month as Insights.
