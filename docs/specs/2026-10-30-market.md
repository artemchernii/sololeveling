## The screen (match the mockup)

Two columns on desktop: THE WEEK and SECTORS ~⅔, THE TREND and RATES AND
PRICES ~⅓ sticky. Phone: one column in the same order. No emoji anywhere
on the tab: line icons, company logos, letter chips (US, EU, JP, EM, CN,
BR, €$).

1. **Head**: "The market · week of <Monday>", the read's week headline
   and two plain sentences; model · web searches · time · tokens · cost ·
   who paid, ↻ READ AGAIN. Before a read: "Not read yet", "Facts only ·
   no stories before ✦ READ MARKET", the ✦ READ MARKET button.
2. **THE WEEK**, "<n> stories · <Mon>–<today>": 3–5 stories, one tile
   each, everything on one left edge after a 40px icon:
   - kind in caps with its icon and colour: INTEREST RATES, EARNINGS,
     EUROPE, GOLD, EMERGING MARKETS, MARKETS, NO DATA; source and day on
     the right;
   - title, one plain sentence (the model's); one plain line under it
     (the model's);
   - three boxes: label · number · the two values or the date it came
     from ("Alphabet in a month · −16.8% · $201 → $167"), a small chart
     where the number is a price change. Red or green only on a change.
     The numbers are the app's facts (`factKeys`), printed by the app;
   - **YOUR MONEY**: what he has in the story, from his accounts — the
     holdings as logo chips with € and the period's change, and one total
     ("€2,750 in companies that reported", "2% of investments in
     Europe"). Nothing held → a grey row "nothing in Brazil · 0% in
     emerging markets";
   - one move: "Open <name> in Ideas" (opens IDEAS with that line lit)
     plus a plain hint, or No action; ✦ Ask on the right.
   Before a read: the stories the app can build from facts alone, with no
   model words and no move — rate decisions this week (Fed, ECB), held
   companies that reported (EARNINGS, the move since the close before,
   `earn-…` facts) and the biggest monthly mover among gold, Stoxx 600,
   Bitcoin and EUR/USD.
3. **SECTORS** (S&P 500 · biggest 30, 1M): one row per sector, best month
   first — line icon, name, a bar from the zero line (full at 12%), the
   %; under it the companies as logos with their %, held ones framed,
   HELD badge in the head. **REGIONS** under it, same rows without
   companies: US, Europe, Japan, Emerging, China, Brazil.
4. **THE TREND** (right): the eight markets with 1M · 3M · 1Y, 3M first;
   each row mark, name, "<close then> → <now>", a small line, the change.
   The US 10-year in points and in ink (a rate, not a gain). HELD on S&P
   500, Nasdaq 100 and gold when held. Head "<day> close → now".
5. **RATES AND PRICES** (right): US inflation, euro inflation (with the
   month before), Fed rate and ECB rate with the next meeting and days to
   it. A source not answering → NO DATA on that box only.
6. Footer: "Prices from Yahoo Finance, fetched on open, kept 10 minutes.
   Rates from the Fed and the ECB, inflation from BLS and Eurostat."

## Data

- **Fetched, never stored** (Insights rule): add 3M to `PERIODS`
  (`range: '6mo'`, 91 days); add `^BVSP` (Brazil shares) and `BRL=X` to
  the action's fetch; Brazil joins the regions. Same one action call on
  open, same 10-minute page cache. No new reactive query.
- **The read** gains `week`: 3–5 stories `{ kind, title, line, factKeys,
  move: { ticker } | null }` and `weekHead`, `weekTake`. Kinds from a
  fixed list in `src/lib/marketRead.ts` that pick the icon. Numbers only
  from facts; the move's ticker must be a line on the IDEAS board or the
  story shows No action. Stored with the read like `lines` (it is the
  read, not market data).
- **YOUR MONEY** is computed by the app, never the model: each story kind
  maps to a set of holdings (earnings → companies that reported, Europe →
  European holdings and euro cash, gold → gold holdings, emerging →
  holdings in those regions, rates → cash in euros) from
  `aggregate.positions` and the cash totals already on the page.
- **Cost**: the same READ MARKET press, a longer answer (≈ +1K output
  tokens). Two more Yahoo symbols per open. No new Convex reads.

## Scenarios (become tests)

S1 Busy week (canned read, 5 stories): head shows the week headline;
5 story tiles, each with kind, title, 3 boxes, YOUR MONEY, a move or No
action; no emoji character in the tab's text.
S2 Earnings story: YOUR MONEY lists the held companies that reported,
each with €, and the total; "Open Alphabet in Ideas" opens IDEAS with
GOOGL lit.
S3 Nothing held: the Brazil story shows the grey "nothing in Brazil"
row and No action.
S4 Not read yet: "Not read yet", stories from facts only, no move
buttons, ✦ READ MARKET present.
S5 Quiet week (canned read, 2 stories, no moves): every story says No
action; tab badge "2 STORIES".
S6 NO DATA: Eurostat not reached → euro inflation box reads NO DATA in
the story and in RATES AND PRICES, nothing else marked.
S7 THE TREND: 3M on open; 1Y changes every row's two values and change;
US 10-year in points, never red or green.
S8 A move to a ticker not on the board → No action (unit test of the
mapper).
S9 Sectors sorted best first; held companies framed; regions include
Brazil.
S10 Phone: one column; no horizontal scroll at 375px.

Convex tests: the read's `week` validator (refuses an unknown kind, more
than 5 stories, a factKey not in facts). Unit tests: YOUR MONEY mapper
per kind, 3M base close, the BRL/BVSP parsers.
