## More free sources (30 Oct, his "do it")

Added after the first "Go": five free sources, all fetched on open by the
same one Convex action, kept on the page 10 minutes, **never stored**
(Convex reads stay as they are). The mockup's **sources** switch tags
every block with where its numbers come from. Not Finviz: no free API,
and scraping it breaks its terms.

| Source | Key | Convex env | Limit | Where it shows |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Finnhub (free) | yes | `FINNHUB_API_KEY` | 60 calls/min | EARNINGS story: a share's result, actual and expected; YOUR COMPANIES: RESULTS (earnings calendar, actual vs expected), ANALYSTS (rating changes), news for held companies |
| SEC EDGAR | no key; a contact in the User-Agent (SEC rule) | `SEC_USER_AGENT` | 10 requests/s | YOUR COMPANIES: INSIDERS (form 4: who, sold or bought, $, day), BIG FUNDS (13F: a fund's change in shares, quarter) |
| FRED | yes, already set | `FRED_API_KEY` | 120/min | RATES AND PRICES: US unemployment, US 2-year and 10-year bond rates; Fed rate as now |
| ECB Data API | no | — | — | ECB rate, euro inflation (cross-check with Eurostat), EUR/USD reference rate |
| Reddit API (personal "script" app) | yes | `REDDIT_CLIENT_ID`, `REDDIT_CLIENT_SECRET` | 100 calls/min | ON REDDIT (right column): top 5 tickers named in the week's top 100 posts of r/stocks, r/investing, r/wallstreetbets; `1,240× this week` with `410× last week` under it; HELD on his own. No move, not a fact for the read's moves, never in `verdict()` or IDEAS |

He creates the accounts: Finnhub (finnhub.io → free key), Reddit
(reddit.com/prefs/apps → "script" app; name, redirect `http://localhost`),
and the SEC contact is his name and email as text. The session sets each
with `npx convex env set <NAME>` on dev and prod and checks one name at a
time, yes or no — never prints a value.

**YOUR COMPANIES** (new tile under THE WEEK): one row per held company
with news this week — logo, name, € held; RESULTS, ANALYSTS, INSIDERS,
BIG FUNDS, each a value and its source and day, "—" where nothing. Phone:
the company on its own line, the four values two by two.

Calls per open, at most: Finnhub 1 calendar + 1 per held company with
news (≤ 15) + 1 rating changes per held company (≤ 15); EDGAR 1 per held
company (form 4 feed) + 1 per fund on a fixed list of 5 (13F); Reddit 1
token + 3 listings; FRED 3; ECB 2. All in parallel, each with its own
NO DATA on failure.

**Open questions (ask him before the build):**

1. **Reddit's "last week"** cannot be fetched: Reddit gives this week's
   top posts, not last week's. Showing it means storing one count per
   ticker a week (5 rows a week). Recommendation: show this week only
   and the rank, store nothing; ask before adding the 5 rows.
2. **Finnhub's rating changes** (`/stock/upgrade-downgrade`) may be
   paid-only. Check with his key first; if paid, ANALYSTS shows the
   month's count of buy / hold / sell (`/stock/recommendation`, free),
   this month and last month side by side.
3. **13F is quarterly** and 45 days late: BIG FUNDS says the quarter
   ("13F · Q3"), never "this week".

## Data

- **Fetched, never stored** (Insights rule): add 3M to `PERIODS`
  (`range: '6mo'`, 91 days); add `^BVSP` (Brazil shares) and `BRL=X` to
  the action's fetch; Brazil joins the regions. Same one action call on
  open, same 10-minute page cache. No new reactive query.
- **The read** gains `week`: 3–5 stories `{ kind, title, line, factKeys,
  move: { ticker } | null }` and `weekHead`, `weekTake`. Kinds from a
  fixed list in `src/lib/marketRead.ts` that pick the icon. Numbers only
  from facts; the move's ticker must be a line on the IDEAS board or the
  story shows No action. Stored with the read like `lines` (it is the
  read, not market data).
- **YOUR MONEY** is computed by the app, never the model: each story kind
  maps to a set of holdings (earnings → companies that reported, Europe →
  European holdings and euro cash, gold → gold holdings, emerging →
  holdings in those regions, rates → cash in euros) from
  `aggregate.positions` and the cash totals already on the page.
- **Cost**: the same READ MARKET press, a longer answer (≈ +1K output
  tokens). Two more Yahoo symbols per open. No new Convex reads.

## Scenarios (become tests)

S1 Busy week (canned read, 5 stories): head shows the week headline;
5 story tiles, each with kind, title, 3 boxes, YOUR MONEY, a move or No
action; no emoji character in the tab's text.
S2 Earnings story: YOUR MONEY lists the held companies that reported,
each with €, and the total; "Open Alphabet in Ideas" opens IDEAS with
GOOGL lit.
S3 Nothing held: the Brazil story shows the grey "nothing in Brazil"
row and No action.
S4 Not read yet: "Not read yet", stories from facts only, no move
buttons, ✦ READ MARKET present.
S5 Quiet week (canned read, 2 stories, no moves): every story says No
action; tab badge "2 STORIES".
S6 NO DATA: Eurostat not reached → euro inflation box reads NO DATA in
the story and in RATES AND PRICES, nothing else marked.
S7 THE TREND: 3M on open; 1Y changes every row's two values and change;
US 10-year in points, never red or green.
S8 A move to a ticker not on the board → No action (unit test of the
mapper).
S9 Sectors sorted best first; held companies framed; regions include
Brazil.
S10 Phone: one column; no horizontal scroll at 375px.
S11 YOUR COMPANIES: a held company with results, a rating change, an
insider sale and a 13F change shows all four with source and day; a
company with none is not listed.
S12 ON REDDIT: 5 rows, HELD on held tickers, no move button; Reddit not
answering → NO DATA on that tile only.
S13 A source without its key (Finnhub, Reddit) → its blocks say NO DATA,
the rest of MARKET loads.

Convex tests: the read's `week` validator (refuses an unknown kind, more
than 5 stories, a factKey not in facts). Unit tests: YOUR MONEY mapper
per kind, 3M base close, the BRL/BVSP parsers.
