# Targets

Journey (31 Oct, his "yes to all"): salary lands or cash piles up at a
broker, once or twice a month. He wants to see his split now against
the split he wants, and what this cash should buy to close it — "give
ideas to ADD some stocks, to add gold or buy BONDS … why that much
money". His targets, in euros, not percent: "crypto … maybe up to 2k
max, 3-5k gold, 5k bonds, and rest are stocks".

Screens/files: Portfolio's WHERE THE MONEY IS card (`PortfolioMap.tsx`)
gets a fourth lens, TARGET, beside KIND · SECTOR · REGION. A new
`targets` table (one row per bucket: min, max), a pure
`src/lib/targets.ts` (buckets, the split), `convex/targets.ts` (read,
save). Journey map: `docs/journeys/finances.md`.

Buckets: STOCKS (stocks and every ETF that is not bonds or gold, with
its own range since his "rest is a bit weird", 31 Oct) · GOLD (metals) · BONDS (ETFs whose name says bond,
treasury or govt) · CRYPTO. Cash is not a bucket: FREE CASH is the
Treasury's free cash, as the hero shows it.

Scenarios (each becomes one e2e test):

- S1 no targets → TARGET → the four buckets with NOW € and SET, no split
- S2 targets set → each bucket: NOW €, TARGET €min–max, a band with the
  now mark; inside → ✓ IN TARGET; under → TO ADD € (min − now); above →
  ABOVE · € max
- S3 cash → TO PLACE € → split: buckets under their min, in proportion
  to what each misses. Every line shows the € and the bucket's now and
  target
- S4 more cash than is missing → the rest is CASH · STAYS (every target
  met → all of it)
- S5 press a bucket → its held positions with their ADD / HOLD / WAIT;
  none held → 0 HELD and ✦ ASK on that bucket
- S6 edit → min and max per bucket, stocks too → save:
  saving… at once, landed tick, closes; a failed save keeps the numbers
  and says NOT SAVED
- S7 phone width, dark theme

Data: NOW is the sum of the bucket's valued positions in euros (source
1/2, as the KIND bar already sums). The target is his own stored number
(`targets`, like `goals.targetValue`). TO ADD is target min minus now;
the split divides free cash by those amounts. Both numbers always on
the line, nothing stored. **Needs a CLAUDE.md line** (his "yes to all",
31 Oct, call 2).

Out of scope: the watchlist (next slice), alerts, selling, IDEAS using
the targets (later: its model gets the split as a fact).
